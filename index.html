Common questions

Before you book

Is the quote really free?
Yes. We come to your home, measure, show samples and give you a written price. There is no charge and no obligation.
How long does it take from order to install?
Most custom orders are manufactured and installed within two to three weeks, depending on fabric availability. We confirm the timeline before you commit.
Do you have child-safe options?
Yes. Cordless and motorized options remove loose cords entirely, and corded products are installed with tension devices to current safety standards.
Can you match blinds in a strata or rental building?
Yes. We regularly match exterior-facing colours to strata requirements and can supply documentation if your building needs it.
